Hey, Diary

September 28, 2026
Hey, Diary, Today was one of those days when my mind had a lot to say. I made this little website, and honestly, seeing my own corner of the internet come together felt special. I even went up to the terrace and tried looking for the moon. Sometimes, little things like that make a day feel different. There were moments when I felt anxious, and moments when I felt guilty about things I had said or done. I kept thinking about them, wondering whether I should have handled things differently. I know I cannot change every moment, but I can learn from it and try to be kinder. *There’s nothing to be afraid of. Everything will be alright. And somehow, it will be.* I want to stop carrying every little worry around with me. I want to let myself breathe, learn, and grow at my own pace. *I don’t care about anyone.* Well, maybe what I mean is that I don't want to let every little opinion decide how I feel about myself. **Be happy.** I hope tomorrow feels a little lighter. I hope I remember that I am allowed to make mistakes and still keep moving forward. **See you soon, Diary. Goodnight.**
